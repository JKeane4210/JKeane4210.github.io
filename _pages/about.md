Hello. I am Jonathan Keane, but everyone calls me Jonny.

I am a Computer Science PhD student at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Ananth Grama. My research interests lie at the intersection of mechanistic interpretability and AI safety because I believe that better understanding of the mechanisms that drive/emerge in these models are a path towards making sure they are safe/reliable. Regarding safety, I look at this issue from a couple different perspectives. One view of this is the concern about how systems using models/networks of models can produce misaligned states and how we can make guardrails to limit this. Secondly (and potentially more important to me), there is the consideration of how human interactions with these models can affect people over time (cognitive offloading, AI psychosis, etc.). These tools should be made very carefully to help people become better versions of themselves, which is extremely important (even if not as glaring) as concerns about misaligned AI models.
